# I&A Fashions storefront — guidance for Claude Code

Static React storefront (Vite + Tailwind) on GitHub Pages; Shopify will be the commerce backend, as on iana-schoolwear.
**The page model is a lookbook**: big pictures edge to edge, few words, colour first. It must not look like the other I&A shops (Schoolwear is an order form, Formal a tailor's catalogue, Parfums a gallery wall).

## Rules
1. **Facts come from `src/data/site.ts`; products from `src/data/catalogue.json`.** Never invent prices, policies, delivery terms, fabric claims, stock levels, reviews or measurements. Confirmed facts (range, fabrics: cotton, jersey, polyester; £18 to £65; delivery and returns) live in `SITE`. Unconfirmed items go in `SITE.toConfirm` and appear on pages only as a body-size `[PLACEHOLDER]`, never in a heading. While `SITE.preview` is true, products are samples and stay labelled: "sample price" on the product page, "sample pieces" in the count line of a listing and beside each rail or list of prices.
2. **Commerce goes through `src/lib/store.ts` only.** Components never fetch product data directly.
3. **Every page sets `<Seo>`.** Add new routes to `scripts/prerender.mjs`.
4. **Pictures.** No generated lifestyle scenes, no faces, no streets, cars or courtyards. A piece is shown by, in this order: its photograph cut to cloth and garment (`public/img/looks`, `public/img/products`; the crops are made by `/home/claude/builds/v4/iana-fashions/_scripts/crops.py`), a drawing in its own colour (`HijabArt`, `CoordArt`, `Drape` in `src/components/Art.tsx`) on Oat warmed with that colour (`tint()`), or a `PhotoSlot` that says what will be shot. No photo twice on one page. The social card (`public/img/og/og-home.jpg`) is a screenshot of the home field (`_scripts/og.cjs`).
5. **Colour world: pink.** Blush `#fff4f2` ground; Light Pink `#ffcad4` for whole fields (listing and page heads, the Eid band, the bespoke form, the footer, the phone buy bar); Pomegranate `#8a2d43` for buttons and the active state only; Oat `#efe5d8` behind pictures; Ochre `#45353a` text; white for the buy panel and form fields. No Sage, no brown or dark bands, no gradients, no shadows.
6. **Shape.** Corners are square. **No rules and no borders anywhere**: sections are separated by space, a field of colour or a picture. The only outlined object is the folded colour chip (`FoldedChip`, outlined in the text colour of whatever it sits on). Buttons are solid Pomegranate blocks in sentence case; sizes are text keys that fill only when chosen; no pills, badges, cards, accordions, caps labels, eyebrows, middle-dot strings or arrows on links.
7. **Type.** Cormorant Garamond 500, very large and tight, for headings and product names; Instrument Sans 400/500 for everything else (change `--font-serif` / `--font-sans` only). **The italic is for colour names and nothing else** (`.colour-name`: "Blush" on the home field, "in blush" under a product name, the colour in a listing's count line). One fluid scale in `index.css` (`--t-hero`, `--t-page` = h1, `--t-section` = h2, `--t-sub` = h3, `--t-name`, `--t-lede`); never hard-code a heading size in a page.
8. **Voice.** Warm, second person, short: "Pick a colour." No slogans, no "X, sorted", no three-part lists for rhythm, no em-dash asides, and say each fact once per page.
9. **Made to fit you:** every abaya can be taken up or amended in the shop (`/alterations`); bespoke is coming soon (register interest saves on the device only, `src/lib/interest.ts`). Abayas come in one length: never reintroduce Petite/Regular/Tall or length pickers. Pre-orders (Eid, new collections) are marked in words ("pre-order" after the name or the price).
10. Tap targets ≥ 44px, no horizontal overflow at 375px, body text ≥ 16px (14px only for the legal line), AA contrast, one h1, British English, GBP.

## Design system (v4)
- **Header** (`Layout.tsx`): the lockup in the middle, Hijabs and Abayas to its left, Co-ords and Eid pre-order to its right. It is fixed, lies transparent over the first field of the page and turns Blush once the page moves; the first block of every page takes `.first` so its field runs up behind it. Over a dark colour field (`html[data-top="dark"]`, set by the colour wall) it uses the white lockup. Phones have no menu button: the categories (with New this week) are a swipe rail under the lockup. There is no strip. Alterations, Visit, Delivery and Everything live in the footer.
- **Signature, the colour field** (`ColourWall.tsx`, Home): the first screen is a field in the chosen hijab colour (`fieldFor()` moves the colour a step so the drape stands off it and the text passes AA). Every hijab colour (`store.hijabWall()`) is a folded chip; choosing one repaints the field, redrapes the `Drape`, names the colour in italic and links to the styles in it.
- **Home, in order:** colour field; "Hijabs in cotton, jersey or polyester." where each fabric is the link (hijab shoppers choose fabric first); "New this week" as a rail of 2:3 pictures running off the screen; Abayas as two pictures, the second set 120px lower, with the alterations sentence between; Co-ords as a pair of pictures running to the right edge; Eid pre-order on Light Pink with the embroidered cloth down one side. No "visit us" band: the footer does that.
- **Product unit** (`Tile` in `Piece.tsx`): a bare picture, edge to edge, and one line under it (name, price). No meta line, swatches or badges.
- **Listing** (`Shop.tsx`): a Light Pink head with the title; hijabs add fabric as text choices (`?fabric=`); then the **colour ribbon**, the one filter, across the full width and stuck under the header (`?colour=`); then the wall (`.wall-pics`: two columns on phones, three from 900px, heights alternating 2:3 and 4:5 so it is uneven). With no colour chosen, `store.dealColours()` gives each drawn hijab a different colour so the wall shows the range.
- **Product page** (`ProductPage.tsx`): pictures stacked on the left and scrolled by the page, a white panel stuck on the right: name, "in blush", price, chips, sizes as text keys, the call button, the alterations line on abayas, one delivery and returns line. Details follow as plain paragraphs. Phones: the pictures swipe sideways, then the panel; the Light Pink buy bar (`.buybar`) shows whenever the main button is off screen and hides at the footer.
- **Footer:** a Light Pink field, centred: one large line, address and hours, the links as a run of words, the delivery and returns sentence, the legal line.
- **Motion:** tokens `--ease-out`, `--dur-1/2/3`. Motion answers an action (pick a colour, the buy bar, the header turning Blush). The one unprompted moment is the hero drape arriving (`.drape-in`). Card to product page is a shared-element change: links are `VLink`, the picture carries `pieceVT(handle)`. Reduced motion switches all of it off.
