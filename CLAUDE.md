# I&A Fashions storefront — guidance for Claude Code

Static React storefront (Vite + Tailwind) on GitHub Pages; Shopify will be the commerce backend, as on iana-schoolwear.

## Rules
1. **Facts come from `src/data/site.ts`; products from `src/data/catalogue.json`.** Never invent prices, policies, delivery terms, fabric claims or measurements. Confirmed facts (range, fabrics: cotton, jersey, polyester; £18 to £65; delivery and returns) live in `SITE`. Unconfirmed items go in `SITE.toConfirm` and appear on pages only as a body-size `[PLACEHOLDER]`, never in a heading. While `SITE.preview` is true, products are samples and must stay labelled as such.
2. **Commerce goes through `src/lib/store.ts` only.** Components never fetch product data directly.
3. **Every page sets `<Seo>`.** Add new routes to `scripts/prerender.mjs`.
4. **No AI-generated faces.** Models are shown from behind or cropped at the shoulders.
5. **Brand (I&A guide 2022, Fashions variant):** Ochre #45353a text and dark areas; Light Pink #ffcad4 as surface/highlight only, never text; Sage #d8e2dc bands and product backdrops; warm white #fbfbf9 ground. Cormorant Garamond 500 for headings and product names (sentence case), its 500 italic as the second voice (colour names, the line under a heading); Instrument Sans 400/500/600 for text and controls (stands in for Agrandir; change `--font-sans` only). Caps 0.15em labels only on controls, table heads and footer headings, never as eyebrows above headings. Corners 3px. No pills, gradients as decoration, drop shadows, card grids for prose, middle-dot strings or arrows on links.
6. **Made to fit you is the core idea:** every abaya can be taken up or amended in the shop (`/alterations`); bespoke is coming soon (register interest saves on the device only, `src/lib/interest.ts`). Abayas come in one length: never reintroduce Petite/Regular/Tall or length pickers. Hijabs are the biggest category: styles are products, colours are real swatches, and until photography arrives a hijab is drawn in the chosen colour (`src/components/Art.tsx`). Pre-orders (Eid, new collections) use the `Pre-order` badge.
   Imagery: no photo twice on one page, the hero frame is never a product card, and a product without its own photo gets a `PhotoSlot`.
7. Tap targets ≥ 44px, no horizontal overflow at 375px, body text ≥ 16px, one fluid type scale (tokens in `index.css`), British English, GBP.

## Design system (v3)
- **Type:** one fluid scale in `index.css` (`--t-hero`, `--t-page` = h1, `--t-section` = h2, `--t-sub` = h3, `--t-lede`, `--t-body`, `--t-small`); classes `.t-hero`, `.t-sub`, `.t-card`, `.voice`, `.voice-lg`, `.lede`, `.small`. Never hard-code a heading size in a page.
- **Signature, the colour wall** (`src/components/ColourWall.tsx`, home hero): every hijab colour (`store.hijabWall()`) as a folded fabric chip (`FoldedChip`); choosing one redraws the `Drape`, tints the stage, names the colour in Cormorant italic and links to the styles in it. On phones the wall is a two-row rail under the headline. The same chip is the swatch everywhere: product pages (`Swatches`, the colour reads under the title as "in blush") and the listing filter "In this colour" (`/shop/<handle>?colour=Name`, which also redraws hijab cards). Cards themselves carry no controls.
- **Motion:** tokens `--ease-out`, `--dur-1/2/3`. Motion answers an action (pick a colour, open the menu, the phone buy bar). The one unprompted moment is the hero drape arriving (`.drape-in`). Card to product page is a shared-element change: links are `VLink`, the picture carries `pieceVT(handle)`. Reduced motion switches all of it off.
- **Home, in order:** colour wall; "New this week" as a snap rail running off the right edge; abayas with the alterations promise as a full-bleed Sage split; co-ords as a pair of photographs; Eid pre-order as a full-bleed Light Pink band; the shop.
- **Product page:** picture left and sticky from `lg`, buy column right; the alterations sentence sits under the size picks on abayas; on phones a buy bar (`.buybar`) shows the price and the call action once the main button has scrolled away and hides at the footer.
- **Listing:** collection chips, colour and fabric filters above the grid, a live count that names the filters, and an empty state with a way out.
- **Header and footer:** one strip plus one header; the phone menu is a full panel with the nav, phone number, address and hours. Footer: brand line, three columns, the delivery and returns line, the legal line.
